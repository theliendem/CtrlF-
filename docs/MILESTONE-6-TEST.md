# Other sites: Chrome check

Reload **Ctrl F++** at `chrome://extensions`. Open a course website that isn't Canvas (for example a department course page with a schedule and lecture-note links) and open the panel with **Cmd+Shift+F**.

1. Search a phrase you know is on a page one or two links deep. Expect the dig to continue past the current page (status reads **Digging <hostname>…**), with the matching page turning green.
2. Only same-site links are read. With **Show skipped external links** on, off-site links appear as gray stubs and are never opened. Login, logout, account, help, cart and image links never appear.
3. A same-site PDF, PPTX or DOCX link is read like a Canvas file: icon, **Page N** or **Slide N**, and **Open at page N** opens the PDF at that page. Clicking the result body opens the page that linked to it, with the link highlighted.
4. **JavaScript-built pages:** on a site whose pages are rendered by JavaScript, a minimized window may briefly appear in the Dock while one page at a time is read. It must close by itself within about 12 seconds, never take focus, and the page's text should be searchable.
5. Clicking a green page opens it in a new tab with the phrase highlighted.
6. Canvas digs should behave exactly as before.

Report the site, the query, and anything that opened, focused, or stayed open unexpectedly.
