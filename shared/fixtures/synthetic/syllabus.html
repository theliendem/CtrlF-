Course Syllabus

Course overview

CS 999 introduces search, crawling and ranking. Lectures meet Tuesdays and Thursdays from 1:00 to 2:15 PM in Room 1240 of the Computer Sciences building.

Office hours are held Wednesdays from 3 to 5 PM in Room 4310, or by appointment over email.

Grading

  • Homework: 30% of the final grade.
  • Two midterm exams: 20% each.
  • Final project: 30% of the final grade, presented in the last week of class.

Late work

Assignments submitted after the deadline lose 10% per day. Nothing is accepted more than five days after the due date.

Each student has three free extension days for the semester that can be used on any homework without asking first.

Collaboration

You may discuss problems with classmates, but every line of code you hand in must be written by you. List anyone you worked with at the top of your submission.

Exams

Both midterms are in class. Rules about allowed materials for each exam are in the Exam Policies (PDF) under Week 1 in Modules.

Missing an exam for a documented illness or family emergency can be excused. Contact the instructor before the exam if at all possible.

Accommodations

Students with a McBurney VISA should send it during the first three weeks so that testing arrangements can be made in time.
